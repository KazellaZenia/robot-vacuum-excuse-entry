Built for [make.fun's "The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-the-robot-vacuum-s-best-excuse-53412214) ($2 in USDC).

One line, at most 140 characters, giving the funniest excuse a robot vacuum could offer for missing a spot:

> I did not miss the spot. My map now lists it as a pet, and I do not eat pets.

`main.py` (pure stdlib) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
